Squish Squish Splat

Privacy Policy

Developer: Shooty Queness
Contact: savemhq@gmail.com
Effective date: November 3, 2026

This policy explains how information is handled when you use Squish Squish Splat, its Android app, and its browser version. Shooty Queness is the developer responsible for the game.

The main points: Your game progress and preferences stay on your device. There are no player accounts or global leaderboards. The Android app includes Google services for advertising, consent, and optional purchases; these services can process information as explained below. You can play without making a purchase.

1. Game progress and device storage

The game stores your current run, personal best, lifetime taps, explosion count, story position, recent reactions, and audio preferences locally. The browser version uses browser storage. Android uses its local WebView storage and private app preferences, including a cached record of whether ads have been removed.

We do not operate a game server that receives your scores, story progress, or purchase receipts. There is no cloud save or player profile. Game progress does not transfer automatically between devices. Clearing the app's storage, clearing browser site data, or uninstalling the app can erase local progress.

2. Advertising and consent

The Android app integrates Google AdMob to display banner ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable consent and privacy choices. Development or internal-testing builds may use test ads. The browser game does not include the native Android advertising SDK.

When Google's advertising and consent services run, Google may process information such as:

  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location.
  • Device and app identifiers, including the Android advertising ID where available and permitted.
  • Device, operating-system, and app information; diagnostic and performance information.
  • Ad requests, impressions, interactions, and signals used for advertising measurement, security, and fraud prevention.
  • Your consent selections and other applicable privacy signals.

Google uses information to provide, personalize where permitted, measure, and protect its advertising services, subject to applicable choices and Google's policies. Information can be transmitted to Google and participating advertising partners for these purposes. We do not request precise GPS location, contacts, camera, or microphone access for gameplay. Google may estimate location from your IP address without GPS permission.

Where required, the app presents Google's consent message before requesting ads. Where Google requires an ongoing privacy-options entry point, Ad privacy appears in the game's music and sound settings. That control lets you revisit the available choices. You can also manage advertising identifiers and advertising privacy in Android settings; available controls vary by Android version. Declining personalization does not necessarily eliminate all ads.

See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps and sites that use its services for details. Google's processing and retention are governed by its policies and the choices available to you.

3. Remove Ads purchases

The Android app supports an optional one-time Remove Ads purchase through Google Play when the product is available. Google Play handles checkout and payment information. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

The app uses purchase information returned by Google Play, such as the product identifier, purchase token, purchase state, package identifier, signature, and acknowledgment status, to verify and acknowledge completed purchases and restore ad-free access. A cached entitlement is stored on your device for offline use. Pending payments do not grant ad-free access.

Restoration and ownership checks communicate with Google Play. Purchase and transaction records may also be available to us through Play Console for order support and refunds. Google's payment and transaction retention rules apply to those records. Removing ads stops this app from requesting banner ads for the verified entitlement; it does not erase records already held by Google or remove information required to maintain your purchase.

4. Support messages

If you email savemhq@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever information you include. We use that information to respond, investigate the issue, and maintain necessary support records. Please do not send passwords, full payment details, or other information unrelated to your request.

Support email is handled through Gmail, a Google service. You may contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any records we need to keep for legal obligations, transaction issues, or unresolved disputes.

5. This website and external services

The browser game and this policy are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard request information, such as IP addresses and technical request logs, to deliver and secure the website. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. This policy page does not contain advertising or analytics scripts.

Google and GitHub may process information on systems in countries other than your own, as described in their privacy policies. External websites and platform services have their own terms and privacy practices.

6. Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local gameplay information remains on your device until it is replaced through normal play or you clear the relevant app/browser data or uninstall the app. Clearing game data does not cancel a Google Play purchase or delete Google's transaction records. Use Google Play's purchase and account controls for those records.

We do not maintain a cloud gameplay database to delete or an account-deletion process, because the game has no player accounts. For personal information we receive directly, such as support emails, contact us to request access, correction, or deletion. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights to object to processing, restrict processing, withdraw consent, or contact a privacy regulator. Requests concerning Google's or GitHub's independently held information should also use their privacy controls or contact channels.

7. Children's information

Playing does not require a name, email address, account, or user profile. We do not knowingly ask children to submit personal information directly to us. Please avoid including children's personal information in support requests. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact savemhq@gmail.com so we can review and delete it as appropriate. Advertising and consent services are subject to the applicable audience settings and platform requirements.

8. Security and policy updates

We use local device storage for game information and the Google-provided services described above for advertising, consent, and purchases. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

We may update this policy when the game's features or information-handling practices change. The effective date at the top identifies the current version. If you have privacy questions, contact Shooty Queness at savemhq@gmail.com.
